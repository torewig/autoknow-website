Team

AutoKnow is based at the Department of Political Science, University of Oslo. The core team will consist of the principal investigator, two postdoctoral fellows, and two PhD fellows, working with associated researchers and co-authors in Norway and abroad.

Core team

University of Oslo

Tore Wig

Principal Investigator · Professor of Political Science, University of Oslo

Tore Wig works on political regimes, political violence, and democratisation, combining historical analysis with quantitative and causal-inference methods. He has published widely on institutions in dictatorships, repression, the measurement of democracy and autocracy, and the role of universities in autocracies. He received the Fridtjof Nansen Award for young researchers in 2024 and the King's Gold Medal for the best PhD dissertation at the Faculty of Social Sciences in 2016.

Open positions

AutoKnow is recruiting two postdoctoral fellows and two PhD fellows at the Department of Political Science, University of Oslo. We are looking for researchers interested in political regimes, the politics of science and higher education, and computational social science, including text-as-data, large language models, and causal inference with observational data.

  • Work with a large, original bibliometric and survey infrastructure
  • Co-author papers within a well-funded, five-year research programme
  • Join a strong community on political regimes and computational social science at Oslo

How to apply

Vacancies are announced through the University of Oslo's recruitment portal. Informal enquiries are welcome at any time.
